About the Club

Welcome to Knight Warriors

Welcome to Knight Warriors!

Knight Warriors is a sports & e-sports club created for passionate sportspersons & gamers who want to take their skills to the next level. Whether you're into competitive play, improving your gameplay, or just enjoying games with a community of like-minded individuals, our club provides a platform for it all.

Our mission is to foster a supportive and engaging environment where members can participate in tournaments, practice together, and share gaming knowledge. We focus on teamwork, skill development, and most importantly, having fun while playing the games we love.

With regular events, tournaments, and practice sessions, Knight Warriors offers a space where players can grow, compete, and represent the club at various levels. As part of the community, you'll have access to gaming facilities, coaching, and a network of fellow gamers who share your drive for esports.

Join us, level up your skills, and be part of a club that's dedicated to bringing the best out of every player!

Our Mission

  • Foster a supportive and engaging environment for every member
  • Run tournaments where members compete and represent the club
  • Practice together and share gaming knowledge
  • Build teamwork and develop real skill
  • Have fun playing the games we love

Membership

Choose Your Rank

Three ways to be part of Knight Warriors — from casual play to representing the club in tournaments.

Standard Membership

Open to all gamers. Access to regular events, practice sessions and club resources, for a monthly fee.

Requirements

  • Anyone passionate about gaming
  • Agree to the club rules
  • Attend meetings and events
  • Pay the monthly fee

Benefits

  • Club tournaments and casual events
  • Use of equipment and facilities
  • Social and community events
  • Learn from experienced players and mentors
Competitive

Competitive Team Membership

Selected to represent the club in tournaments, with regular team practices and scrimmages. Members must meet skill and rank requirements.

Requirements

  • Pass tryouts or demonstrate your skill
  • Follow structured practice schedules
  • Work closely with team captains

Benefits

  • Everything in Standard
  • Exclusive team practice sessions
  • Represent the club at local and regional tournaments
  • Coaching and game analysis

Honorary / Support Member

For alumni and former members who stay involved — assisting with events, mentorship and resources, with no regular participation required.

Requirements

  • Former competitive players, or
  • Past contributors to the club

Benefits

  • Access to special events
  • Support the club through mentorship, without active play

Practice Schedule

Train Like a Warrior

Structured sessions for competitive squads, relaxed play for casual teams — and skill work for everyone.

Competitive Teams

2–3 sessions / week

2–3 hours each, captain and coach-led.

  • Team meeting, then warm-up
  • Scrimmages and game reviews
  • Post-session debrief
  • Attendance tracked by captains — members expected at most sessions

Casual Teams

1–2 sessions / week

Flexible attendance — play at your own pace.

  • Basic teamwork and mechanics
  • Social play with fellow members
  • Light scrimmages and theme nights

Skill Development

Weekly drills & review

For every member who wants to level up.

  • Individual skill drills — aim, reflexes, micro-management
  • Weekly strategy and game review — replays, positioning, decision-making
  • Communication drills — callouts and real-time decisions
  • Rotating scrimmages — weekly in-house, monthly against partner clubs

Rules & Code of Conduct

The Warrior's Code

Every member of Knight Warriors Club agrees to these rules — on behaviour, sportsmanship and representing the club, in practice, at events and in tournaments.

  • Respect fellow members; always maintain a respectful tone, online or offline.
  • No toxicity: harassment, bullying, or toxic behaviour will not be tolerated.
  • Good sportsmanship: win or lose, treat opponents and teammates with respect.
  • Attend meetings, practice sessions and club events regularly; inform the team captain or event coordinator if you cannot attend.
  • Actively participate in events, tournaments and discussions.
  • Stay committed to improving your skills and contributing to club activities.
  • Work as a team: cooperate, share knowledge and help others improve.
  • During competitive play, follow the team captain's guidance and strategies.
  • No cheating: cheating, hacking, or using exploits is strictly prohibited — violation means immediate disciplinary action.
  • No account sharing during club activities or tournaments.
  • Behave professionally and represent Knight Warriors positively at all events.
  • Be punctual for matches, tournaments and events.
  • Obey the rules set by external event organisers.
  • Promote the club positively; post no negative or harmful content.
  • Public posts, announcements and collaborations under the club's name must be approved by the PRO or the committee.
  • Pay membership dues on time.
  • Treat club-owned equipment (PCs, consoles) with care; report damage immediately to the Technical Coordinator.
  • Warnings (verbal or written) for minor violations.
  • Suspensions for repeated or serious misconduct.
  • Expulsion for severe breaches, such as cheating or harassment.
  • Regularly check club communications — email and group chats.
  • Give feedback constructively.
